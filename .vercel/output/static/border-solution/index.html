Automated Patrols

The drones run automated patrol routes day and night, in darkness and bad weather. Thermal cameras spot people moving through dense vegetation or river crossings before ground units are aware.

AI Recognition

AI identifies what the drone sees in real time — people versus animals, group size, direction of travel — so operators receive confirmed alerts, not hours of raw footage to review manually.

Target Intercept

When a contact is detected, ground units get GPS location, movement direction, and a live aerial feed before they deploy. Intercept teams arrive informed, not blind.

Automated Logging

Every detection is automatically logged with time, location, and classification — creating a verifiable record for legal proceedings and long-term pattern analysis.
